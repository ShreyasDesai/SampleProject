## Post

Most teams only check search. AI answers read your site too.

SEO (search engine optimisation) covers search. ASO (app store optimisation) covers app stores.

GEO (generative engine optimisation) and AIO (AI Overviews optimisation) cover the third place: whether AI answers can find, read and cite your pages.

AI answers cite pages they can extract. Three checks you can run yourself:

1. Ask an AI answer engine the question your customers ask most. Note whose pages it cites.

2. Open your robots.txt. Make sure the pages you want read are not blocked.

3. Test one live URL for extractability. Lumen checks a live URL for on-page and AI extractability signals.

Today: put your customers' most common question to one AI answer engine and write down who it cites.
