## Post

Most teams chase new keywords before they check their own site.

Good SEO (search engine optimisation) starts with pages that can be crawled at all.

Three things to check first:

1. robots.txt: is a Disallow line blocking pages you want found?

2. Sitemap: are your important pages in it?

3. Broken pages: do any of your links lead to an error?

Fix what is broken. Then go after new keywords.

Lumen crawls your public pages, robots.txt and sitemap without an API key.

Today: crawl your own domain in Lumen and start with whatever is broken. [VERIFY: public Lumen URL not set yet]
