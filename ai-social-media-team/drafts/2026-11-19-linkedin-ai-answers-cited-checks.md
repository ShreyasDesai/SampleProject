## Post

Would ChatGPT or Perplexity cite your site if someone asked them your reader's best question?

In plain words, that is what GEO (generative engine optimisation) and AIO (AI Overviews optimisation) are about: being found and cited in AI answers, not only ranked in search results.

I'm still learning how each engine picks its sources, so I won't guess. But you can check where you stand.

1. Ask ChatGPT and Perplexity the question your reader would ask. Write down which sites they cite.

2. Search the same question on Google. If an AI Overview appears, note which pages it links to.

3. Open your robots.txt and check you are not blocking the crawlers these engines use. [VERIFY: the crawler names each engine uses, and whether blocking one changes what gets cited]
